Keyboard: focus order

  1. Close (1). On a failed save, focus moves to the error summary (role=alert); its links (2 to 3) jump to the invalid fields.
  2. Then in order: Supplier status radios (one Tab stop, arrow keys inside), Share, Contract end date, the four Strategic-intent checkboxes, Notes, Save, Cancel.
  3. Errors are written next to the field, with a heavy ink rule and aria-invalid; never colour alone. Nothing is required: an empty form saves an empty context.

Keyboard: keys

  • Tab in reading order; Space toggles checkboxes; arrow keys select in the status group.
  • Enter in a single-line field saves.
  • Visibly not AI content: white fields, a solid ink rule on the saved context, no AI badge and no provenance.
