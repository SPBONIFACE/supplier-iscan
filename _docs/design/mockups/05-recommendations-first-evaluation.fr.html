Keyboard: focus order

  1. Close (1), then “Add buyer context” (2), before the recommendations, because it changes what follows.
  2. Each recommendation, in reading order: cited-fact links, Provenance (open on the first), Approve, Correct, Delete.
  3. The relationship level, the volume share and the priority are written in the meta line (text), before the citations.

Keyboard: keys

  • Tab / Shift+Tab in reading order; Enter or Space activates buttons and toggles Provenance.
  • A cited-fact link moves focus to that fact row on the card; the browser Back returns. The level and the volume share are written, never colour alone.
  • Tokens only, from tokens.css. No horizontal scroll at 400px: buttons wrap, the meta line wraps.
