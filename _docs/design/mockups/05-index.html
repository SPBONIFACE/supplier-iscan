For the owner

  • Financial guidance is built by rules, so it cannot honestly carry "AI draft, pending your validation" (spec F8 says every statement does). Mock-up: dashed "Pending your validation" with provenance "Rules, not a model". Decide the label.
  • Natural-person business: the mock-up keeps business-capability SWOT points and replaces risk and financial content. Alternative: no assessment at all.
  • Access-status wording for the seven financial document statuses (EN/FR), proposed in 05-assessment-ai-draft.html; the brief does not list them.
  • Side panel background: paper, not paper-margin (so the margin note keeps its tone).
  • Margin note behaviour: 300px column beside the fact from 1440px wide; inline under the row below that.
  • Source-content deletion date in the header: "Scheduled for <date>" / "Deleted on <date>"; when the date is null (no retention set) the header item is left out, no date invented.
  • French card values shown in French; "About people" field note; "n facts still proposed" and "n statements pending" counts; banner sentences: all proposals.
  • Concurrent fact edit: "Apply my correction" overwrites the other member's correction after they have seen it.

Conventions

  • Evidence status ("Found in <source> on <date>", Source type, Redacted extract, missing evidence) sits in the source line and the margin note. Buyer review status (Proposed, Approved, Corrected; AI draft, Approved, Corrected, Deleted) sits at the right of the value or under the statement. They never share a shape or a place. The word "verified" is not used for a review.
  • Brick red appears only on "No public evidence found" and on Evidence gaps.
  • Fonts: IBM Plex from Google Fonts in these mock-ups only; the application self-hosts them (#31).
