What this page takes from each reference

  • Tearsheet theme: paper and ink colours with one green accent, Barlow Semi Condensed headings, Piazzolla body, rules instead of boxes, no bold values, numbered notes and every source listed at the end.
  • Your Market Analysis card: the band of five figures, head office / group / latest strategic move, strengths next to risk watch-points, the procurement-implication bar, and the row of contract approach, volume share and next actions.
  • Beroe: a one-page snapshot that starts with who the company is; product lines and named customers; a four-part assessment (we keep strengths, risk watch-points, opportunities and evidence gaps, and invent no weakness).
  • spend-ai: “Recent events”, a dated timeline, newest first, undated items last and never called latest.
  • Supplier iScan’s own: a note number on every value, the quote one click away (open on Revenue), “No public evidence found”, supplier’s own vs third-party, Group / This entity tags, review actions on every fact and statement.
