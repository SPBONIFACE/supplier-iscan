Keyboard: focus order

  1. Close (1); the marker banner comes first, its single action “Redraft recommendations” (2) is described by the line beside it (aria-describedby).
  2. In the drafting state the primary button is disabled (dashed, still in reading order) and “Cancel redraft” (3) follows; a polite live region announces start and end. Then Edit buyer context, then the recommendations as usual.
  3. Reviewed (approved) recommendations are never replaced; unreviewed AI drafts that will be replaced say so. Their citations still say version 2 until redrafted.

Keyboard: keys

  • Tab / Shift+Tab in reading order; Enter or Space activates buttons and toggles Provenance.
  • A cited-fact link moves focus to that fact row on the card; the browser Back returns. The level and the volume share are written, never colour alone.
  • Tokens only, from tokens.css. No horizontal scroll at 400px: buttons wrap, the meta line wraps.
