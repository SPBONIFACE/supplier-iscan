Keyboard: focus order

  1. 1 Strength 1, Correct pressed: the text becomes a field, focus inside. 2 Save correction. 3 Cancel. Focus returns to the statement.
  2. Weakness 1, Delete pressed: a confirmation replaces the actions; focus moves to 5 Delete statement; 6 Keep it. Esc = Keep it.
  3. After deletion, focus moves to the next statement (or the subsection heading if none is left).
  4. 7 Opportunity 1 (corrected): cited fact, then Provenance (open, shows the AI text).
  5. 10 History of deleted statements (disclosure).

Keyboard: keys

  • In the statement field Enter adds a line (statements can be several sentences); Ctrl/⌘+Enter saves; Esc cancels.
  • Empty text on Save: 2px ink border and "Error: enter the statement text." under the field; nothing is sent.
  • Delete always asks for confirmation; Esc keeps the statement.
  • A 409 conflict shows the current statement and keeps the member's text, as in 05-review-conflict.html.

States and criteria shown

  • Correct: inline editing, cited facts kept, AI text kept in provenance.
  • Delete: confirmation step; afterwards the statement disappears from the section and from the PDF.
  • Deleted statements: only in history, DELETED label (ink-tertiary, solid), struck-through text, who and when.
  • Corrected statement's provenance: provider, model, generation time, review status and the original AI text.

For the owner

  • Deleted statements shown in a "History" disclosure at the end of the section: confirm, or keep them only inside each statement's provenance export.
