Keyboard: focus order

  1. Skip link (first stop on the page), then top bar links. When the card opens, focus moves to the supplier name.
  2. 1 Close panel.
  3. 2 Website link (new tab).
  4. 3 "Go to the next proposed fact": moves focus to that row.
  5. 4 Company overview fact list: one Tab stop, on its active row (here Employees); ↑ ↓ move between rows.
  6. 5 Employees source chip (open). 6 Close quote. 7 Source URL in the note (new tab).
  7. 8 Production footprint fact list (one stop, as 4).
  8. 9 Certifications fact list, active row Quality management. 10 its chip. 11 "Verify on IAF CertSearch" (new tab).
  9. Environmental certification (No public evidence found) is reached with ↓; then Tab reaches its "Verify on IAF CertSearch" link.
  10. Then the assessment section (see the assessment mock-ups).

Keyboard: keys

  • Tab / Shift+Tab: next / previous stop, in the order on the left.
  • Each section's fact list is one Tab stop (roving tabindex); ↑ ↓ move between its rows; Home / End: first / last row.
  • From a row, Tab reaches its source chip, then the links and controls inside the row.
  • Source chip: Enter or Space opens the margin note and moves focus to its heading; Esc closes it and returns focus to the chip.
  • Esc with no note open closes the panel and returns focus to the workspace row that opened it.
  • When the panel opens, focus moves to the supplier name (heading, tabindex=-1).

States and criteria shown

  • Header: name, website, country, sector, last refresh, source-content deletion date (filled by #45; three cases in the index), count of facts still proposed.
  • One row per field: value, Fact status (Proposed / Approved, with byline), source chip.
  • Row source line: "Found in <source> on <date>" (the chip), Source type label, "Redacted extract".
  • Margin note open on Employees: Quote in French (source language), Plex Serif blockquote, attribution: author (a person's name, shown as […]), publisher, URL, date, link.
  • "No public evidence found" (Subcontracted processes, Environmental certification), brick, no chip, no status.
  • Certification fact with "Verify on IAF CertSearch"; also kept on the missing certification.
  • Management field flagged concerns_people: names appear only as […]; field note says it is never used in the assessment.
  • Wide screens (1440px and more): the note opens in a 300px margin column beside its fact. Narrower: inline under the row. Resize the window to compare.

For the owner

  • The side panel uses paper, not paper-margin, so the margin note, flag banners and the panel do not share one tone. The brief's token description lists the side panel under paper-margin: confirm.
  • The "About people" field note on concerns_people fields is a proposal; the spec does not ask for it.
  • Source-content deletion wording: "Scheduled for <date>", and after deletion "Deleted on <date>" (see 05-supplier-card-flags.html).
