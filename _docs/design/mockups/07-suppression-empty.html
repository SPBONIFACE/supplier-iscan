Keyboard focus order

Black numbered markers on the screen show the sequence of Tab stops; the list groups them.

  1. Skip to content.
  2. Top bar: Organisations, Usage log, Errors, Provider settings, Suppression list; language; Sign out.

  5. Entries: Remove… on each row.
  6. Footer links.

Annotations

  • Administrator only. The screen shows no supplier name, fact, Quote or other profile content: only the entry, its reason, date and who added it.
  • Person names and domains are invented placeholder values (a person-name entry is personal data).
  • Editing is not offered: to change an entry, remove it and add a new one.
  • Footer: the privacy-notice and legal-notices links appear in the footer of every page (members, administrators, demo and public pages, issue #7). Document texts are owner-supplied (#41).
